## Keep a Role From Deploying

Every role may deploy by default. To withhold it, set the `deploy` permission to `false` in the role's blueprint:

```yaml [site/blueprints/users/editor.yml]
title: Editor

permissions:
  johannschopplich.deploy-trigger:
    deploy: false
```

Users of that role don't see the button, and the server refuses their deploy request.

A role blueprint that sets `"*": false` switches `deploy` off too. To let that role deploy, set `deploy: true` under `johannschopplich.deploy-trigger` next to the wildcard.
