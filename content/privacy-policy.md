## Visiting the Website

When you visit this website, your browser necessarily sends data to the server, including your IP address, the requested address, the page you came from (referrer) and details about your browser and operating system. The server needs this data to deliver the page to you. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure and working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The website is delivered by Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, through its worldwide server network. Cloudflare processes the connection data on our behalf (Art. 28 GDPR), also to fend off attacks. We receive no log of page visits from Cloudflare. In the process, the data may reach the USA and other countries outside the EU. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for which an adequacy decision of the European Commission exists (Art. 45 GDPR), and has additionally agreed to the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). Learn more in [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

You are not obliged to provide us with any data. Without the connection data, however, the website cannot be displayed.

## Storage on Your Device

When you visit the website, we set no cookies and store nothing on your device. We serve fonts and scripts ourselves, not through third-party providers.

When you switch between the light and dark design, your browser stores your choice locally (local storage) so that it persists on your next visit. This setting never leaves your device. This storage is strictly necessary for the function you requested (Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG, the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act).

Cookies or similar technologies only come into play when you click the buy button or sign in to the customer portal. The details are in the following sections.

Links to other websites are ordinary links. Only when you click one does your browser connect to the other website; its own privacy policy applies there.

## Buying Licenses

We sell our licenses through Paddle. Your contractual partner for the purchase is Paddle.com Market Limited, Judd House, 18–29 Mora Street, London EC1V 8BT, United Kingdom, which acts as the merchant of record. Paddle processes your payment, billing and contact data as an independent controller; [Paddle's privacy policy](https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy) applies to this. Paddle's checkout loads only when you click the buy button; Paddle may use cookies or similar technologies needed for the payment. For the United Kingdom, an adequacy decision of the European Commission exists (Art. 45 GDPR).

After your purchase, we receive from Paddle the details we need for your license: your email address, the order number, the purchased product and the license holder you entered. We use them to create, verify and manage your license (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The license data is stored in a database at Cloudflare (see above); we send emails about your license, such as sign-in links, through Resend from Plus Five Five, Inc., San Francisco, USA, which is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR). Both act on our behalf (Art. 28 GDPR). We keep the license data for as long as your license exists, and beyond that as far as tax retention obligations require (§ 147 AO, German Fiscal Code).

You manage your licenses in the customer portal hub.kirby.tools, which also runs on Cloudflare. You sign in there with your email address, either through a sign-in link we send you via Resend or together with your order number. After you sign in, we set a session cookie that keeps you signed in for seven days; it is strictly necessary for the portal you requested (Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG). There, too, Paddle's checkout loads only when you buy an upgrade.

## Contact by email

When you contact us by email, we process your details – such as your name, contact details and the content of your message – to handle your request. If it concerns a contract or an order, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; for other enquiries, it is our legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Providing your details is voluntary; without them, however, we cannot answer you.

Emails to our address are received by Cloudflare (see above) and forwarded to our mailbox. Our mailbox is run by IONOS SE, Elgendorfer Str. 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany, in data centres in Germany.

We delete your request as soon as it has been dealt with. We keep it longer only where the law requires it – for example six years for business letters under § 147 AO (German Fiscal Code).

## Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

- access the data stored about you (Art. 15)
- rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16)
- erasure of your data (Art. 17)
- restriction of processing (Art. 18)
- data portability (Art. 20)
- object to processing (Art. 21, see below)

Simply contact us, for example by email at [hello@kirby.tools](mailto:hello@kirby.tools). We do not use automated decision-making or profiling.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is the Saxon Data Protection and Transparency Commissioner, Postfach 11 01 32, 01330 Dresden, Germany, [www.datenschutz.sachsen.de](https://www.datenschutz.sachsen.de).

## Right to Object

Where we process data on the basis of our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you may object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21(1) GDPR). We will then no longer process the data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, or the processing serves the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.
