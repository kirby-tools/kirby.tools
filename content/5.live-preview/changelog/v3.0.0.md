::tip
This release runs on Kirby 6. Sites on Kirby 4 or 5 stay on their current major.
::

Kirby Live Preview v3 runs on Kirby 6 only and rerenders the preview once the editor pauses typing. It is included in Generation 3 licenses; with a Gen 1 or Gen 2 license you receive a 50% discount on the upgrade. Head over to the [Kirby Tools Hub](https://hub.kirby.tools) or read the [Kirby 6 upgrade guide](/upgrading-to-kirby-6).

**🚨 Breaking Changes**

- **Kirby 6 required**: The plugin runs on Kirby 6 and PHP 8.3, 8.4, or 8.5. Stay on v2 for Kirby 4 and 5.
- **Live Preview section is a field**: Move `livePreview` from `sections:` to `fields:` in your blueprints – until then, Kirby shows `Invalid section type ("preview")`. The type and every property keep their names, and a sticky column still works. See [Configuration](/docs/live-preview/configuration).
- **Rerenders after a typing pause**: The preview rerenders once the editor stops typing for `updateInterval` milliseconds, 500 by default, instead of every 500 milliseconds while they type. See [Configuration](/docs/live-preview/configuration#updateinterval).
- **`updateStrategy` is removed**: The typing pause covers what `blur` was for. Kirby ignores the property, so you can delete it from your blueprints.
- **`interactable: 0` means `false`**: The preview turns inert for `0` as it does for `false`.
- **`pageId` follows page access**: A page the editor's role can't access shows **Preview failed** instead of rendering. See [Troubleshooting](/docs/live-preview/troubleshooting).
