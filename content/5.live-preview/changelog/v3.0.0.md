Kirby Live Preview v3 is a **compatibility release** for Kirby 6 and runs on Kirby 6 only. It ports v2 without new options. It is included in Generation 3 licenses; with a Gen 1 or Gen 2 license you receive a 50% discount on the upgrade. Head over to the [Kirby Tools Hub](https://hub.kirby.tools) or read the [Kirby 6 upgrade guide](/upgrading-to-kirby-6).

**🚨 Breaking Changes**

- **Kirby 6 required**: The plugin runs on Kirby 6 and PHP 8.3, 8.4, or 8.5. Stay on v2 for Kirby 4 and 5.
- **Live Preview section is a field**: Move `livePreview` from `sections:` to `fields:` in your blueprints – until then, Kirby shows `Invalid section type ("preview")`. The type and every property keep their names, and a sticky column still works. See [Configuration](/docs/live-preview/configuration).
- **Invalid values show an error**: An `updateStrategy` other than `interval` or `blur` shows an error with the plugin's message in place of the field, as an invalid `updateInterval` or `aspectRatio` does. v2 fell back to `interval`. See [Configuration](/docs/live-preview/configuration).
- **`interactable: 0` means `false`**: The preview turns inert for `0` as it does for `false`.
- **`pageId` follows page access**: A page the editor's role can't access shows **Preview failed** instead of rendering. See [Troubleshooting](/docs/live-preview/troubleshooting).
