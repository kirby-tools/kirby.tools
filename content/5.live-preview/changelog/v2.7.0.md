**🐞 Bug Fixes**

- **Previewing another page**: With [`pageId`](/docs/live-preview/configuration#pageid) set to a page other than the one you edit, the preview no longer fills that page with your unsaved content. It shows the page as saved.
- **Jump links**: Links like `#team` now scroll to their section inside the preview instead of loading the home page.

**🔒 Security**

- **Messages from the preview**: The Panel now saves or follows a link only when the preview itself asks, and never opens a script link like `javascript:`. Prevents an embed on the previewed page, such as a third-party widget, from saving your changes.
