**🐞 Bug Fixes**

- **Edits during a slow render**: When you edit while the preview is still rendering, the preview now shows your last change. Before, that change could stay missing until your next edit.
- **Unsaved content in other languages**: With a language other than the default one selected, the preview now shows your unsaved changes instead of the saved page.
