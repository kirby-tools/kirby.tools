::tip
This release runs on Kirby 6. Sites on Kirby 4 or 5 stay on their current major.

Kirby Content Translator v4 is a **major release** for Kirby 6 and runs on Kirby 6 only. Translation, the view button, and the Translation Coverage in the **Languages** view are unchanged from v3. It is included in Generation 3 licenses; with a Gen 1 or Gen 2 license you receive a 50% discount on the upgrade. Head over to the [Kirby Tools Hub](https://hub.kirby.tools) or read the [Kirby 6 upgrade guide](/upgrading-to-kirby-6).

**🚨 Breaking Changes**

- **Kirby 6 required**: The plugin runs on Kirby 6 and PHP 8.3, 8.4, or 8.5. Stay on v3 for Kirby 4 and 5.
- **Content Translator section is a field**: Move `contentTranslator` from `sections:` to `fields:` in your blueprints. `systemPrompt`, `cascade`, and every other property keep their names. See [Field Configuration](/docs/content-translator/configuration/local#field-configuration).
- **Import without confirmation**: The `confirm` option is removed, so **Import** fills the current language on the first click. The imported fields arrive as unsaved changes, and **Discard** reverts them.

The config, hook, and YAML changes for developers are in the [migration guide](/docs/content-translator/getting-started/migration#migrating-from-v3-to-v4).
