**🐞 Bug Fixes**

- **Field names in any spelling**: [`includeFields`](/docs/content-translator/configuration/local#includefields), [`excludeFields`](/docs/content-translator/configuration/local#excludefields) and `fieldTypes` now match a name like `metaDescription` regardless of its casing. On the view button and in the global config, anything but all-lowercase previously matched nothing, and those fields were silently left untranslated.
