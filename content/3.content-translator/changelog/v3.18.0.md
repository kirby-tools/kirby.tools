**🚀 Features**

- **Translate language variables**: The **Translator** button can now be added to Kirby's language view, where it translates the language's variables, or those of several languages at once. See [Language Variables](/docs/content-translator/panel/language-variables).

**🐞 Bug Fixes**

- **Text in parentheses is translated**: A parenthetical that starts like a KirbyTag, such as `(Note: closed on Sundays)` or `(10:00 – 18:00)`, kept its source text. Only a tag type Kirby has registered, its own or a plugin's, now counts as a KirbyTag.
