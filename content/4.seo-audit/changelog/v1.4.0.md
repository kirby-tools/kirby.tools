**🚀 Features**

- **Synonyms field**: [`synonymsField`](/docs/seo-audit/configuration/local#synonymsfield) option to add synonyms to the page's keyword or keyphrase.
- **Custom SEO checks**: Three [custom checks](/docs/seo-audit/guide/assessments#kirby-seo-audit-custom-assessments) complement Yoast's assessments. They flag images without an `alt` attribute, a missing or repeated H1, and headings that skip a level.
