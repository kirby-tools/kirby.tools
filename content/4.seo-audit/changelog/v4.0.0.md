Kirby SEO Audit v4 is a **major release** for Kirby 6 and runs on Kirby 6 only. The Yoast SEO assessments and the rating are unchanged from v3. It is included in Generation 3 licenses; with a Gen 1 or Gen 2 license you receive a 50% discount on the upgrade. Head over to the [Kirby Tools Hub](https://hub.kirby.tools) or read the [Kirby 6 upgrade guide](/upgrading-to-kirby-6).

**🚨 Breaking Changes**

- **Kirby 6 required**: The plugin runs on Kirby 6 and PHP 8.3, 8.4, or 8.5. Stay on v3 for Kirby 4 and 5.
- **SEO Audit section is a field**: Move `seoAudit` from `sections:` to `fields:` in your blueprints. `keyphraseField`, `synonymsField`, `persisted`, and every other property keep their names. See [Field Configuration](/docs/seo-audit/configuration/local#field-configuration).
- **Stored reports start over**: The last report a section kept in the browser doesn't show in the field. The next analysis stores it again. Ratings carry over.
- **Single braces resolve**: `keyphrase` and `synonyms` read `{ page.title }` as a query, like `{{ page.title }}`, so a literal `{…}` in them is replaced. See [Shared Properties](/docs/seo-audit/configuration/local#shared-properties).
