**🚀 Features**

- **Improved proxy configuration**: The [`proxy`](/docs/seo-audit/configuration/global#proxy-configuration) option replaces `transformers.proxyUrl`. Its `urlResolver` rewrites the URL the proxy fetches, for headless setups or custom domains. Its `params` add headers, authentication, or a timeout to the request.
