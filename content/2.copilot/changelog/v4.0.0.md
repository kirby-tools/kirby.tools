Kirby Copilot v4 is a **major release** for Kirby 6 and runs on Kirby 6 only. It ports v3 without new features. It is included in Generation 3 licenses; with a Gen 1 or Gen 2 license you receive a 50% discount on the upgrade. Head over to the [Kirby Tools Hub](https://hub.kirby.tools) or read the [Kirby 6 upgrade guide](/upgrading-to-kirby-6).

**🚨 Breaking Changes**

- **Kirby 6 required**: The plugin runs on Kirby 6 and PHP 8.3, 8.4, or 8.5. Stay on v3 for Kirby 4 and 5.
- **Copilot section is a field**: Move `copilot` from `sections:` to `fields:` in your blueprints – until then, Kirby shows `Invalid section type ("copilot")`. The type and every property keep their names. See [Copilot Field](/docs/copilot/usage/section).
