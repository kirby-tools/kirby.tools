**🐞 Bug Fixes**

- **Only your prompt resolves**: [Placeholders](/docs/copilot/prompt-dialog/placeholders), [page references](/docs/copilot/prompt-dialog/page-references), and [skill references](/docs/copilot/prompt-dialog/skills) resolve in your prompt, no longer in the selected text or in a field value a placeholder inserts.
- **AI translation leaves your content intact**: [AI translation](/docs/content-translator/providers/ai-translation) in Content Translator keeps `{0}`-style placeholders and `@page://` or `@skill://` references in your content as written.
