**🚀 Features**

- **Custom user prompts**: Define a [`userPrompt`](/docs/copilot/usage/toolbar-buttons#predefined-user-prompts) on a `writer` or `textarea` field to pre-fill its Copilot dialog with instructions for that field. Works with nested fields in blocks and layouts.
