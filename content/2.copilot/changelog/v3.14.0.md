::tip
Agents are here! Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or any other agent with remote MCP support to your site – it prepares the changes, you publish them.
::

**🚀 Features**

- **Agents**: An AI agent outside the Panel can now read your content and prepare changes to it. You approve each connection in the Panel, and the agent works as your Kirby user, within your role. By default, its edits wait as unsaved changes until you publish them. See [Connect an Agent](/docs/copilot/agents).
- **Agents view**: A new Panel view shows the MCP URL you add to your agent and checks whether agents can reach your server. It lists your connections with their permissions and when each was last active, and lets you change a connection's permissions or revoke it. See [Manage Connections](/docs/copilot/agents#manage-connections).
- **Connection permissions**: Every connection can read; preparing, publishing, and deleting are yours to allow. Role blueprints can keep publishing and deleting out of agents' reach altogether. See [Permissions & Review](/docs/copilot/agents/permissions-and-review).
- **An open page reloads**: When an agent writes to a page you have open, the view reloads within 10 seconds and tells you why, so you see its changes without refreshing. See [Working Alongside an Agent](/docs/copilot/agents/permissions-and-review#working-alongside-an-agent).

**🐞 Bug Fixes**

- **Prompt dialog in the writer**: Opening the prompt dialog from the writer while text is selected no longer crashes the browser tab in Chromium-based browsers.
