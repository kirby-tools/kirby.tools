**🚀 Features**

- **Kirby queries in the view button**: [`userPrompt`](/docs/copilot/configuration/local#userprompt) and [`systemPrompt`](/docs/copilot/configuration/local#systemprompt) now resolve Kirby queries for the view button, not just for the section. A prompt like `{{ page.customPrompt.value }}` follows the content instead of reaching the model verbatim.

**🐞 Bug Fixes**

- **Unresolvable page references**: A page reference that no longer resolves is skipped instead of failing the whole generation.
